# Job and investment scams

Fake job offers: fraudsters advertise work-from-home or part-time jobs through WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS or social media. They may ask for a "registration fee", "training fee", "security deposit" or for documents, then disappear. Genuine employers do not charge candidates fees for jobs.

Task-based scams: victims are paid small amounts for simple tasks such as liking videos or writing reviews. They are then added to a Telegram group and asked to "invest" in prepaid tasks for higher returns. Displayed profits are fake and withdrawals are blocked unless more money is paid.

Investment scams: promises of guaranteed or very high returns in stock trading, crypto or forex, often through fake trading apps or websites showing fabricated profits. Fraudsters may impersonate well-known investment advisers.

Warning signs: guaranteed returns, pressure to invest quickly, payments to personal bank accounts or UPI IDs, demands for "tax" or "fees" before withdrawal, communication only through messaging apps.

What to do: stop paying immediately — paying more never unlocks withdrawals. Save chats, group names, UPI IDs, bank account details and transaction IDs, and report to the national cybercrime helpline 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
