# Sanchar Saathi

Sanchar Saathi is a citizen-centric portal of the Department of Telecommunications. Its facilities include:

- Chakshu: report suspected fraud communication received through calls, SMS or WhatsApp — for example impersonation of bank officials, government officials or KYC update scams — even when no money was lost.
- Checking mobile connections issued in your name, and reporting connections you did not take, which helps against identity misuse.
- Blocking a lost or stolen mobile handset.

Note: Chakshu is for reporting suspected fraud communication. If you have already lost money or are a victim of cybercrime, report to the national cybercrime helpline or portal instead.

Vishing (voice phishing) warning signs: callers claiming to be from your bank, police, customs, courier or a telecom company who create fear ("your SIM will be blocked", "a parcel in your name contains drugs", "you are under digital arrest"), demand immediate payment, ask for OTPs, or ask you to stay on a video call. Government agencies do not arrest people over video calls or demand money over the phone. Hang up and verify using official numbers.
