# Smishing (SMS phishing)

Smishing is phishing carried out through SMS text messages. Common lures include an "unpaid electricity bill" with a threat of disconnection tonight, a "parcel delivery failed" notice, a "KYC expired" or "account blocked" warning, reward points about to expire, and fake job or loan offers. The message usually contains a link or a phone number to call.

Warning signs: urgency or threats, links that do not match the organisation's official website, shortened links, requests for card details, OTP or PIN, messages from ordinary mobile numbers instead of an organisation's registered sender ID, and offers that look too good to be true.

What to do: do not click the link or call the number in the message; check directly with the organisation through its official website or app; never share OTPs, PINs or card details. Suspected fraud communication received by call, SMS or WhatsApp can be reported on the Sanchar Saathi portal (Chakshu facility) even if no money was lost. If money was lost, report it to the bank and through the national cybercrime helpline or portal. Keep the SMS and sender number as evidence.
