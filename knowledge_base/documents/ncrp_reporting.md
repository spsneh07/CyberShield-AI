# How to report a cybercrime

The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) is the Government of India's online platform for citizens to report cybercrime complaints. Complaints are routed to the concerned State/UT law-enforcement agency.

For financial cyber fraud (money lost through UPI, card, net banking, wallet or similar), report as quickly as possible by calling the national cyber fraud helpline 1930 or by filing a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in. Reporting quickly matters because the reported transaction details can be used to request that banks and payment intermediaries hold the defrauded funds before they are moved further.

The portal also has a category for reporting crimes against women and children, including online sexual abuse material, which can be reported anonymously.

When filing a complaint, keep ready: a description of what happened, the date and time, the suspect's details used (phone numbers, UPI IDs, website URLs, social media profiles, email addresses), transaction details (transaction ID/UTR number, amount, date, bank) and screenshots or other evidence. After filing, note down the acknowledgement/complaint number for follow-up.

You can also report a cybercrime at your nearest police station.
