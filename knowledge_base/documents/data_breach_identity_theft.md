# Data breaches

A data breach is unauthorised access to, or disclosure of, personal or organisational data, for example a leaked customer database. Leaked phone numbers, emails and passwords are then used for targeted phishing, account takeover and identity fraud. Organisations should report cyber security incidents, including data breaches, to CERT-In.

If your data may have been exposed: change passwords on the affected service and anywhere the same password was reused; enable multi-factor authentication; be extra careful with calls or messages that quote your personal details, since fraudsters use leaked data to sound genuine.

# Identity theft

Identity theft is misuse of someone's personal identity details or documents, for example loans, SIM cards or accounts opened in their name. Check credit reports for loans you did not take, and use the Sanchar Saathi portal (https://sancharsaathi.gov.in) to check mobile connections issued in your name and report ones you did not take. Report misuse to the institution concerned and through the national cybercrime portal or police, keeping copies of any notices, messages or statements.

# Account takeover

If an email, social media or banking account is taken over, use the service's official account-recovery process, change the password, sign out of unknown devices and sessions, review linked phone numbers and email addresses, and enable multi-factor authentication. Warn contacts not to respond to requests for money sent from the compromised account, and keep screenshots as evidence.
