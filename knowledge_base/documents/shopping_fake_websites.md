# Online shopping fraud and fake websites

Fraudsters set up fake shopping websites or social media "stores" offering branded products at very large discounts, or pose as sellers/buyers on classifieds sites. After payment, nothing is delivered, or a cheap counterfeit arrives.

Fake websites may also imitate banks, government portals, electricity boards or courier companies using look-alike domain names (extra letters, different endings) to collect personal and payment details.

Warning signs: prices that are too good to be true, recently created websites, no verifiable address or contact, payment only by UPI/bank transfer to individuals, pressure to pay immediately, and URLs that differ slightly from the official brand.

Safety tips: type known website addresses yourself, prefer cash-on-delivery or protected payment methods on trusted platforms, check reviews from independent sources, and never scan a QR code to "receive" money.

If cheated: report the seller/website to the platform, contact your bank or payment app immediately, keep order confirmations, chats, URLs and payment proof, and report through the national cybercrime portal.
