# Evidence preservation

Good evidence helps police and banks act on a complaint. Preserve evidence as soon as possible and never edit, crop misleadingly or delete it.

What to keep:
- Screenshots of messages, chats, emails and social media profiles, showing the date, time, sender number/ID and profile URL.
- The original messages themselves — do not delete chats, SMS or emails, even after taking screenshots.
- Suspicious links and website addresses (copy the text; do not open them again).
- Phone numbers and call logs of calls from the suspect.
- Bank/UPI transaction details: transaction ID or UTR number, date, time, amount, the beneficiary account/UPI ID, and a bank statement.
- Email headers for email fraud, where possible.
- Any app installed at the fraudster's request (note its name; do not reinstall).

Tips: store copies in a safe place (for example a separate folder or cloud storage), write down a timeline of events while it is fresh, and note down the reference numbers of complaints made to banks, platforms and police.
