# Social media crimes

Impersonation: a fraudster creates a fake profile using someone's name and photos, then messages their friends asking for urgent money. If you are impersonated: report the profile to the platform using its impersonation reporting option, ask friends to report it, warn your contacts not to send money, and keep screenshots of the profile URL and messages.

Account takeover: if your account is hacked, use the platform's official account recovery process, change your password, enable two-factor authentication, review linked email/phone numbers and remove unknown devices or sessions.

Sextortion: criminals record or morph intimate images (often via video calls from strangers) and threaten to share them unless paid. Do not pay — payments usually lead to more demands. Stop contact, do not delete the conversation, take screenshots, report the account to the platform and report the crime to the police or the national cybercrime portal.

Cyberbullying and harassment: do not respond to the abuser, block and report them on the platform, preserve evidence (screenshots with dates, profile links), and report to the national cybercrime portal or police. Reach out to trusted people for support.
