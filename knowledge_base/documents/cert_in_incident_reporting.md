# CERT-In and cyber security incidents

CERT-In is the national agency for responding to computer security incidents in India. It publishes security advisories and vulnerability notes, and receives reports of cyber security incidents such as malware infections, ransomware, website compromise, phishing websites and data breaches. Incident reporting details are listed on the CERT-In website.

Note: CERT-In handles technical security incidents; financial fraud and crimes against individuals should be reported to the police through the national cybercrime reporting portal.

General guidance for malware and ransomware:
- Disconnect the affected device from the internet and network to limit spread, but do not wipe or reset it before evidence is preserved, if an investigation may be needed.
- Do not pay ransoms; payment does not guarantee recovery and funds criminal activity.
- Restore from clean, offline backups after the infection is removed.
- Keep the operating system, browsers and apps updated; install apps only from official stores.
- Remote-access apps (screen sharing tools) installed at a stranger's request should be uninstalled, and passwords changed from a clean device.
