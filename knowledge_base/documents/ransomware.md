# Ransomware

Ransomware is malicious software that encrypts files or locks systems and demands a payment, often in cryptocurrency, for a decryption key. It commonly arrives through phishing email attachments or links, malicious downloads, exposed remote-access services and unpatched software.

If you are affected: isolate the affected device from the network (unplug the cable, turn off Wi-Fi) to limit spread; do not delete or reformat systems before evidence is preserved, as logs, the ransom note and sample encrypted files help investigators; note the ransom note text, any contact addresses and wallet addresses; do not pay the ransom, as payment does not guarantee recovery and funds criminal activity; restore from clean offline backups only after the infection has been removed. Organisations should report cyber security incidents to CERT-In; individuals can report the crime through the national cybercrime reporting portal or police.

Prevention: keep regular offline or offsite backups and test restoring them; keep operating systems and software updated; use reputable security software; be careful with email attachments and links; disable or restrict remote-access services that are not needed; use strong, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication.
